📖 Policy Documentation

Attendance Evaluation Rules:

  • Present: Check-in is recorded on or before Office Start + Grace Period (e.g. 09:15).
  • Late: Check-in is after Grace Period but on or before Half-Day Cutoff (e.g. between 09:16 and 12:00).
  • Half Day: Check-in is after Half-Day Cutoff (e.g. 12:01+), or total shift worked is less than 4 hours upon checkout.

Payroll Deduction Policy:

  • Daily Rate = Monthly Base Salary / Work Days
  • Half days incur exactly 0.5 × Daily Rate deduction.
  • Late arrivals do not incur automatic monetary fines unless configured.
  • Paid leaves do not deduct from salary.
